> [!note] Comment
> The unit °C/W identifies thermal resistance, rather than thermal conductivity. For a specified thermal path, the temperature rise is approximately $\Delta T=\theta P$. A path rated at 40 °C/W produces a 20 °C rise at 0.5 W; the temperatures compared must match that resistance’s stated endpoints.

> [!note] Comment
> A schematic symbol describes electrical terminals; its footprint defines where physical pads and holes go on the board. Correct logic in the schematic cannot compensate for the wrong footprint or an incorrect pin-to-pad mapping. Check package variant and pin 1 orientation together when interpreting the land pattern.
