ASYMPTOTIC NOTATION



Three symbols not to confuse


                  O(g(n))                                  Ω(g(n))       Θ(g(n))
                upper bound                             lower bound   tight bound




     Rule of thumb

     If f(n) is both O(g(n)) and Ω(g(n)), then f(n) is Θ(g(n)).

       double bench(function<void(vector<int>&)> f, int n, int reps=5){
         vector<int> base(n); iota(base.begin(), base.end(), 0);
         double total = 0;
         for(int r=0; r<reps; r++){
           shuffle(base.begin(), base.end(), mt19937(r+7));
           auto A = base; auto t0 = chrono::high_resolution_clock::now();
           f(A); auto t1 = chrono::high_resolution_clock::now();
           total += chrono::duration<double, milli>(t1-t0).count();
         }
         return total / reps;
